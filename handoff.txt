HANDOFF - Custom1 coalition AI (AoE2 DE)
Last updated: 2026-11-02

GOAL
  An AI for the user's custom games. The user (player 1) plays an "overlord":
  Regicide, king/queen units trigger unit creation and infinite resources, and
  usually a 30-minute truce. Players 2-8 are all AIs allied against player 1.
  The AIs should cooperate: find the king, gather, attack together in
  formation, reinforce in groups, and keep building armies after the map's
  resources run out (they get abundant resources).

HOW TO PLAY
  Scenario: Custom1_1v7 (or the original Custom1, which has the same teams).
  Set all 7 AI slots to c1_enemy, and set the truce in the lobby.
  Restart the game after every install, because it reads AI files at startup.
  Proof that it loaded: chat shows "c1_enemy: player N joined the coalition".

COALITION DESIGN (overlord.per, v2)
  - Target and focus are always player 1. The leader is elected through shared goals (heartbeats).
  - King: every AI scans for the king (class 959) on every pass and shares KX/KY/KTIME.
    The leader researches Treason with (spy) only when the king has been unseen for 90s
    (ov-king-stale), and at most once every 180s (ov-spy-every). A sighting stays the
    target for 180s. Otherwise the target is the nearest building, falling back to the
    start position (position-focus) during the truce.
  - Rally point: ov-rally-dist (35) tiles in front of the target, toward the leader's base.
    It only moves if it shifts more than 12 tiles.
  - Phases (shared goal 202): 0 = stock AI only; GATHER = 1; ATTACK = 2; regroup goes back to 1.
    Gathering starts ov-stage-secs (240s) before the truce ends (up-get-treaty-data).
    The attack starts when the truce is over AND (75% of AIs are ready OR 180s have passed).
    Regroup when the wave drops below 40% of its starting size, or after 600s.
  - Orders: the whole army in one up-find-local (all units, cmdid military), excluding the
    king, ships, unpacked trebuchets and garrisoned units; two batches of 240.
      March to the rally: no formation (gets through chokes); idle units re-sent every 5s tick.
      Launch: units within 60 tiles of the target attack-move in line formation.
      Reinforce every 30s: the group waiting at the rally goes in together.
      Re-launch if the king moves more than 12 tiles.
  - While phase >= 1, the stock AI's own army control is switched off: attack groups (0, disabled),
    sn-task-ungrouped-soldiers 0, sn-disable-defend-groups 14, sn-number-tasked-units 0,
    patrol-attack 1.
  - Idle trade carts are deleted at the population cap.
  - Chat: joined / gathering (truce countdown) / ATTACK! wave N / regrouping / Treason used / king spotted.

TECHNICAL NOTES
  Free goals used: 402-417, 421-424, 426-445 (other goals up to 512 belong to Promisory).
  Shared goals: 200-209 for the plan, 210+p heartbeat, 220+p ready, 230+p army,
    240-243 for the king and Treason.
