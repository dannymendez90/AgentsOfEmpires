HANDOFF - Custom1 coalition AI (AoE2 DE)
Last updated: 2026-11-18

GOAL
  An AI for the user's custom games. The user (player 1) plays an "overlord":
  Regicide, king/queen units trigger unit creation and infinite resources, and
  usually a 30-minute truce. Players 2-8 are all AIs allied against player 1.
  The AIs should cooperate: find the king, gather, attack together in
  formation, reinforce in groups, and keep building armies after the map's
  resources run out (they get abundant resources).

HOW TO PLAY (always tell the user the exact setup after each change)
  Single Player > Custom > Scenario: Custom1_1v7
  Player 1: the user. Players 2-8: AI "c1_enemy" (all 7 slots).

COALITION DESIGN (overlord.per, v2)
  - Target and focus are always player 1. The leader is elected through shared goals (heartbeats).
  - King: every AI scans for the king (class 959) on every pass and shares KX/KY/KTIME.
    Treason with (spy): a fixed rota with no shared state. Each 600s slot (ov-spy-every)
    belongs to one AI (P2..P8, repeating), and that AI uses Treason once in its slot. Dead or
    castle-less AIs skip their slot. FIXED BUG: the "Spies" rule (research 408) is Treason in
    Regicide, so every AI was spamming it; it's now limited to non-Regicide games. A sighting stays the
    target for 180s. Otherwise the target is the nearest building, falling back to the
    start position (position-focus) during the truce.
  - Rally point: ov-rally-dist (35) tiles in front of the target, toward the leader's base.
    It only moves if it shifts more than 12 tiles.
  - Phases (shared goal 202): 0 = stock AI only; GATHER = 1; ATTACK = 2; regroup goes back to 1.
    Gathering starts ov-stage-secs (240s) before the truce ends (up-get-treaty-data).
    The attack starts when the truce is over AND (75% of AIs are ready OR 180s have passed).
    Regroup when the wave drops below 40% of its starting size, or after 600s.
  - Orders: the whole army in one up-find-local (all units, cmdid military), excluding the
    king, ships, unpacked trebuchets and garrisoned units; three batches of 240 (500-pop games).
      March to the rally: no formation (gets through chokes); idle units re-sent every 5s tick.
      Launch: units within 60 tiles of the target attack-move in line formation.
      Reinforce every 30s: the group waiting at the rally goes in together.
      Re-launch if the king moves more than 12 tiles.
  - While phase >= 1, the stock AI's own army control is switched off: attack groups (0, disabled),
    sn-task-ungrouped-soldiers 0, sn-disable-defend-groups 14, sn-number-tasked-units 0,
    patrol-attack 1.
  - Idle trade carts are deleted at the population cap.
  - Chat: joined / gathering (truce countdown) / ATTACK! wave N / regrouping / Treason used / king spotted.

SESSION 2026-11-18 (v5) - ROOT CAUSES FOUND
  User: no chat at all in game; Treason never fires. In v2 Treason fired constantly and
    "king spotted" showed. History: v2 and e13829d had an unguarded (research 408) rule, and
    THAT is what produced Treason. 7f47356 restricted it to non-Regicide games and relied on
    the (spy) command, and Treason stopped for good. So (spy)/(can-spy) apparently never
    worked for these AIs; research 408 does.
  Chat: up-chat-data-to-all messages never appeared (in game or in any recording), not even
    the unconditional "joined". Only chat-to-all ("king spotted") ever showed. All chat now
    uses chat-to-all with literal text (per-player rules where the player number matters).
  Treason now: research 408 on a shared clock (shared goal 243), first due at game time
    ov-first-spy = truce-max - 30 = 1770s, then every 900s. It doesn't depend on the phase
    logic or the truce reading. Any AI with a castle (gold topped up to 1000 by cheat) does
    it; the (spy) command is only a backup. The leader chats once if it's 90s overdue.

==== v8 (2026-11-19) ====
Playtest of v7: waves announced and sent, but the bulk of each wave turned back and
clustered in the AI cities; Treason researched on time but never revealed the king
(the king was only found when the player exposed him).
Root cause of the turn-back: Promisory general.per "Actively defending" / "Helping ally"
(goals patroldefense / patrolhelp). Any enemy near ANY AI town -> every non-attacking
soldier of the AI (no distance filter) patrols there, 6s later ALL soldiers are stopped
and up-retreat-now sends the army home. Fixed by patches gen_overlord.py writes into
custom1/c1_enemy/Promisory (marker "; c1_enemy patch"): distance filter
ov-defend-radius (30) on the patrol and stop searches, up-retreat-now -> do-nothing.
v8 changes:
 - waves use patrol (ov-wave-action 2; 19 = attack-move). Every 30s soldiers within
   ov-near-target (40) of the target, not fighting, restart the patrol from where they
   stand (no formation), so the return leg never walks them home.
 - Treason: after research, look the king up by object ID (up-set-target-by-id; checks
   type 434, player 1, hp>0). ID = ov-king-id 544300 (scenario reference id) until any
   AI sees him, then the real object-data-id (shared goal 247). Chat: "Treason located
